# Code of Conduct

FarmFit AI contributors are expected to collaborate respectfully and professionally.

## Expected

- constructive technical feedback;
- respect for different backgrounds and experience levels;
- clear attribution for others' work;
- responsible handling of data and credentials;
- honest representation of what the prototype does.

## Not Acceptable

- harassment or discriminatory conduct;
- deliberately exposing another contributor's private information;
- committing stolen/proprietary material without permission;
- falsifying results, sources or performance claims;
- knowingly publishing secrets or credentials.

Concerns should be raised with the repository owner or hackathon organizers through the available team/event channels.

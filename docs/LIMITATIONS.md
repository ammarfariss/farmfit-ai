# Limitations

FarmFit AI is a decision-support prototype created during Reboot the Earth 2026.

## Current Limitations

- GIS/environmental datasets may be coarser than individual farm plots.
- Remote or model-derived soil data do not replace field soil sampling.
- Mapped water infrastructure does not prove legal or physical water availability.
- Financial estimates depend on assumptions and can vary materially.
- Crop yields are not guaranteed.
- Market conditions and crop prices can change.
- A high optimization score does not guarantee profitability.
- The 2D spatial layout is a planning concept, not an engineering or construction design.
- The system does not provide regulatory, zoning or permitting approval.
- The prototype may support only a limited set of crops and production systems.
- Optimization quality depends on the correctness and completeness of input data.

## Responsible Use

FarmFit AI should support human decision-making, not replace agronomists, engineers, regulators, investors or farmers.
